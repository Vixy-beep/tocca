What the Signature Journey Is All About

Every day unveils a different layer of the Amalfi Coast and beyond - from the pastel villages of Positano and Amalfi to the quiet beauty of Maiori, Minori, Atrani, Tramonti, Praiano, Conca dei Marini, and the island of Capri & Pompeii

You will discover the Coast's flavors, landscapes, and traditions in a soft balance of culture, movement, and pure Mediterranean joy.

This journey is perfect for travelers who want:

  • Authentic connection
  • Curated beauty
  • Zero planning
  • High-touch service
  • A soul-deep travel experience

What Awaits You

Immerse yourself in the authentic soul of the Amalfi Coast

Lemon Groves & Limoncello Making

Walk the terraces with local growers and craft your own limoncello straight from the source.

Olive Groves & Olive Oil Tasting

Savor liquid gold while learning the art of pressing from families who have tended these groves for generations.

Buffalo Mozzarella Experience

Meet master cheesemakers and taste silky buffalo mozzarella made only moments before.

Pizza Making Class

Roll, toss, and fire your own Neapolitan pie alongside a pizzaiolo who knows every secret.

Cooking Class

Create multi-course coastal dishes with a local chef and gather around the table to enjoy them.

Ceramic Making Workshop

Shape and glaze vibrant Vietri-inspired pieces to bring a touch of the coast home.

Farm-to-Table Dinner

Dine al fresco with produce harvested that morning and stories shared by the farmers themselves.

Private Boat Day

Set your own course on a private vessel, stopping for swims in crystalline coves.

Sea Cave Visit

Drift into hidden grottos where emerald light dances across the water.

Sunset Aperitivo

Toast the golden hour with spritzes, local bites, and sweeping coastal views.

Meals on the Sea

Enjoy chef-prepared dishes served on deck while the coastline drifts by.

Sailing Experience

Trim the sails with your skipper and feel the breeze carry you past iconic villages.

Fitness & Wellness

Rebalance with sunrise yoga, coastal hikes, or bespoke spa rituals tailored to you.

Sports Activities

Choose from kayaking, paddleboarding, or adrenaline-fueled cliffside excursions.

Lemon Trail Hike

Follow ancient pathways perfumed with citrus and framed by sweeping sea views.

Vineyard & Wine Tasting

Sip rare vintages in hillside cellars and meet the winemakers behind each bottle.

Vespa Ride

Feel the coastal breeze as you zip past pastel villages on a vintage Vespa.

Pompeii

Walk the storied streets of Pompeii with an archaeological guide illuminating every detail.

Positano

Climb the vibrant stairways of Positano and browse chic boutiques tucked into the cliffs.

Amalfi

Discover Amalfi’s maritime legacy and its striking cathedral overlooking the piazza.

Ravello

Lose yourself in Ravello’s cliffside villas, famed gardens, and open-air concerts.

Atrani

Wander the winding alleys of Atrani, Italy’s smallest seaside village.

Tramonti

Head inland to verdant Tramonti for rustic wineries and slow countryside living.

Conca dei Marini

Take in sweeping vistas and the famed Emerald Grotto in picturesque Conca dei Marini.

Capri Island Day

Sail to Capri for chic piazzettas, hidden coves, and the legendary Blue Grotto.
