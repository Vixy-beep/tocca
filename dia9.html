Day 9 – Ravello’s Romance

Morning free. In the afternoon, ascend to Ravello, a jewel suspended between sea and sky. Stroll the gardens of Villa Rufolo or Villa Cimbrone, with views that inspired Wagner. Evening: a refined dinner in Ravello, with the option of attending a private concert or aperitivo on the Terrazza dell’Infinito.
