Day 7 – The Heart of Amalfi

Spend the day in Amalfi town, visiting the grand cathedral and strolling the medieval alleys. Join a lemon grove tour, learning about the region’s prized sfusato lemons. Taste limoncello and lemon-infused treats. Conclude with a traditional Amalfitano dinner, celebrating the coast’s most iconic fruit.
