Why Choose us?

Tocca Amalfi is more than a travel experience — it's a feeling. One you carry in your skin, your memory, your soul.

Born from my lifelong love for travel, shaped by years living across 7 countries and 5 continents — including two decades in the U.S. working as an executive and director in the travel industry — Tocca Amalfi is the most personal chapter of my journey.

It was here — on the Amalfi Coast — that I found what I never expected: love, family, home, and a sense of belonging. Now a local by heart and by choice, I invite you to experience this coast not as a tourist, but as part of its rhythm.

What We Offer

At Tocca Amalfi, we don't believe in cookie-cutter tours. We design moments — deeply local, thoughtfully curated, and full of soul.

From sipping limoncello in lemon groves to sailing into the sunset with a glass of wine… From winding Vespa rides through cliffside villages to private cooking lessons in family kitchens…

  • 🍋 Taste fresh limoncello under ancient lemon trees

Your Journey Through Time

Explore each magical moment of your Amalfi Coast adventure

🇮🇹
Day 1

Departure

Your Italian escape begins! Board your flight and get ready to immerse yourself in the sun-kissed beauty of the Amalfi Coast.

🏛
Day 2

Arrival & Welcome

Arrive in Naples, private coastal drive, check-in with sea views. Welcome cocktail and dinner on a panoramic terrace — your first taste of la dolce vita.

🍃
Day 3

Flavors of the Coast

Relaxing morning, then hands-on cooking class on a cliffside terrace. Prepare regional recipes and toast with wine as the sun sets.

🛥
Day 4

Pompeii & Pizza

Guided tour of ancient Pompeii ruins. Evening in Tramonti for traditional pizza-making dinner with master pizzaioli.

🌸
Day 5

Enchanting Capri

Sail to legendary Capri. Explore Blue Grotto, elegant boutiques, seaside swim. Romantic dinner with fresh seafood and island wines.

🍋
Day 6

Positano's Magic

Pastel houses cascading to the sea. Ceramic-painting with local artisan. Candlelit dinner by the beach under twinkling lights.

🌤
Day 7

Heart of Amalfi

Grand cathedral, medieval alleys, lemon grove tour. Taste limoncello and celebrate the coast's iconic fruit with traditional dinner.

🏨
Day 8

La Dolce Far Niente

Full free day — lounge by the beach, indulge in spa treatments, or wander the charming village piazzas at your own pace.

🗿
Day 9

Ravello's Romance

Ascend to Ravello, jewel between sea and sky. Villa Rufolo & Cimbrone gardens. Evening dinner with optional concert on Terrazza dell'Infinito.

🍝
Day 10

Wines of the Hills

Full immersion in Tramonti vineyards. Learn winemaking, rustic wine-pairing lunch in countryside. Farewell toast under the stars.

👋
Day 11

Arrivederci

Journey's end after breakfast. Transfer to Naples airport, but memories of the Amalfi Coast stay forever. Until we meet again!
