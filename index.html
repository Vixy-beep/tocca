Why Choose Us?

Tocca Amalfi Coast is more than a travel experience - it's a feeling.
A rhythm that stays with you: in your skin, your memory and your soul.

Each journey unfolds with ease and intention, blending local authenticity, quiet beauty, and seamless care from the first contact to beyond farewell.

Born from my lifelong love for exploration and shaped by years living across seven countries and five continents - including more than two decades in the U.S. leading global teams and transformations in the travel industry - Tocca Amalfi Coast is the most personal chapter of my journey.

It was here, on the Amalfi Coast, that I discovered what I never expected: love, family, home, and a true sense of belonging. Now, as a local by heart and by choice, I created Tocca Amalfi Coast to share the coast as I live it - through its people, its stories, and its quiet, timeless beauty.

I invite you to experience the Amalfi coast not as a visitor, but as part of its soul to touch, taste, and truly feel the Coast.

Benvenuti a casa. Welcome home. Bienvenido a casa
Jessica Pena, Founder & Host

What We Offer

We offer curated moments that invite you to live the Amalfi Coast - effortlessly, authentically, and with soul

Your journey flows through the soul of the Amalfi Coast — from Maiori and Minori to Amalfi, Positano, and beyond — with special visits to Capri, Pompeii, and hidden coastal gems along the way.

From the first hello to beyond farewell, every detail is arranged with intention - creating a seamless and joyful experience that lets you simply arrive and live la dolce vita.

As your personal host, I'll welcome you upon arrival and accompany you along the way - ensuring everything flows naturally, with the perfect balance of support and freedom.
