Why Us?

Because travel should be felt — not just seen.

There are many ways to visit the Amalfi Coast — but only one way to truly feel it.

At Tocca Amalfi, we don't design itineraries; we curate journeys that unfold — slowly, soulfully, and with meaning in every detail.
Each experience is deeply local, beautifully curated, and seamlessly cared for — from the first hello to beyond farewell.

Born from my lifelong love of travel and shaped by years living across seven countries and three continents — leading global teams and reimagining journeys across the world of travel — Tocca Amalfi is the most personal chapter of my story.

It was here, on the Amalfi Coast, that I found what I never expected — love, family, and home.
Now, as a local by heart and by choice, and as your personal host and concierge, I share the Coast as I live it — through its people, its stories, its simplicity, and its timeless beauty.

Every guest becomes part of the story — woven into the rhythm of the Coast itself.

Because this isn't a trip.
It's an invitation — to belong, to breathe, and to feel the soul of the Coast, long after you've left.

Benvenuti a casa. Welcome home.
— Jessica Pena, Founder & Host
