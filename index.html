Why Choose us?

Tocca Amalfi is more than a travel experience — it's a feeling. One you carry in your skin, your memory, your soul.

Born from my lifelong love for travel, shaped by years living across 7 countries and 5 continents — including two decades in the U.S. working as an executive and director in the travel industry — Tocca Amalfi is the most personal chapter of my journey.

It was here — on the Amalfi Coast — that I found what I never expected: love, family, home, and a sense of belonging. Now a local by heart and by choice, I invite you to experience this coast not as a tourist, but as part of its rhythm.

What We Offer

At Tocca Amalfi, we don’t believe in cookie-cutter tours. We design moments — deeply local, thoughtfully curated, and full of soul.

From sipping limoncello in lemon groves to sailing into the sunset with a glass of wine… From winding Vespa rides through cliffside villages to private cooking lessons in family kitchens…

  • 🍋 Taste fresh limoncello under ancient lemon trees
  • 🚤 Sail through golden sunsets with prosecco in hand
  • 🛵 Ride a Vespa through hidden cliffside roads
  • 🍝 Learn to cook with locals who open their kitchens — and their hearts

The 10-Day Journey

An unforgettable itinerary along the coast

Day 1

🇮🇹 Arrival in Naples & The Adventure Begins

Personal welcome, transfer to your boutique hotel, and a welcome dinner.
Day 2

🏛 Pompeii & Volcanic Wines

Tour the ruins and enjoy a wine-tasting lunch on the slopes of Vesuvius.
Day 3

🍃 A Day of Your Own

A day to breathe, feel, and wander. Follow whatever calls to you.
Day 4

🛥 Capri: Glamour, Grottoes & Golden Skin

Private boat trip to the iconic island, exploring its caves and streets.
Day 5

🌸 Citrus Trails & Golden Sips

Walk the "Path of Lemons" and learn the secrets of limoncello.
Day 6

🍋 The Grand Finale in Positano

Free morning to enjoy, followed by a farewell dinner with spectacular views.
Day 7

🌤 Departure & Arrivederci

Leisurely breakfast and a private transfer to Naples airport.
Day 8

🏨Ravello's Gardens & Villas

Explore the stunning gardens and panoramic views of Villa Rufolo and Villa Cimbrone in Ravello.
Day 9

🗿The Path of the Gods

Hike the breathtaking "Sentiero degli Dei" trail with a local guide, offering spectacular views of the coastline.

Day 10

🍝 A Taste of Amalfi: Hands-On Cooking Class

Learn the secrets of local cuisine in a private class, making fresh pasta and regional dishes.
