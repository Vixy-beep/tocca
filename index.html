Why Choose us?

The 10-Day Journey

An unforgettable itinerary along the coast

Day 1

🇮🇹 Departure

Your Italian escape begins! Board your flight and get ready to immerse yourself in the sun-kissed beauty of the Amalfi Coast.
Day 2

🏛 Arrival & Welcome

Arrive in Naples, where a private driver whisks you along the stunning coastal road. Check into your hotel overlooking the sea. In the evening, gather for a welcome cocktail and dinner on a panoramic terrace — the first taste of la dolce vita.
Day 3

🍃 Flavors of the Coast

A slow morning to rest and explore at leisure. In the afternoon, roll up your sleeves for a hands-on cooking class on a cliffside terrace. Prepare regional recipes and toast with wine as the sun sets — your first real taste of the Amalfi way of life.
Day 4

🛥 Pompeii & Pizza in the Hills

Morning free to relax. In the afternoon, step back in time with a guided tour of the ancient ruins of Pompeii, frozen in time since AD 79. Evening: head to Tramonti, birthplace of many master pizzaioli, for a traditional pizza-making dinner.
Day 5

🌸 Enchanting Capri

Sail to the legendary island of Capri. Explore the Blue Grotto, shop the elegant boutiques, and enjoy a seaside swim. As twilight falls, linger for a romantic dinner in Capri, savoring fresh seafood and island wines before returning to the mainland.
Day 6

🍋 Positano’s Magic

Discover Positano's enchanting beauty, craft ceramics with locals & dine by moonlit waters.
Day 7

🌤 The Heart of Amalfi

Explore ancient cathedrals, stroll fragrant lemon groves & savor authentic limoncello.
Day 8

🏨 La Dolce Far Niente

A full free day to enjoy as you wish — lounge by the beach, indulge in a spa treatment, or wander the village piazzas.
Day 9

🗿 Ravello's Romance

Morning free. In the afternoon, ascend to Ravello, a jewel suspended between sea and sky. Stroll the gardens of Villa Rufolo or Villa Cimbrone, with views that inspired Wagner. Evening: a refined dinner in Ravello, with the option of attending a private concert or aperitivo on the Terrazza dell’Infinito.

Ascend to Ravello: stroll Villa Rufolo & Cimbrone; evening dinner with optional concert or aperitivo on the Terrazza dell’Infinito.
Day 10

🍝 Wines of the Hills

A full-day immersion in the vineyards of Tramonti. Wander between vines, learn the art of local winemaking, and enjoy a rustic wine-pairing lunch in the countryside. Return to the coast for a farewell toast under the stars.
