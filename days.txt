Amalfi Coast Journey – 11 Days / 10 Nights

Day 1 – Departure

Your Italian escape begins! Board your flight and get ready to immerse yourself in the sun-kissed beauty of the Amalfi Coast.

⸻

Day 2 – Arrival & Welcome

Arrive in Naples, where a private driver whisks you along the stunning coastal road. Check into your hotel overlooking the sea.
In the evening, gather for a welcome cocktail and dinner on a panoramic terrace — the first taste of la dolce vita.

⸻

Day 3 – Flavors of the Coast

A slow morning to rest and explore at leisure.
In the afternoon, roll up your sleeves for a hands-on cooking class on a cliffside terrace. Prepare regional recipes and toast with wine as the sun sets — your first real taste of the Amalfi way of life.

⸻

Day 4 – Pompeii & Pizza in the Hills

Morning free to relax.
In the afternoon, step back in time with a guided tour of the ancient ruins of Pompeii, frozen in time since AD 79.
Evening: head to Tramonti, birthplace of many master pizzaioli, for a traditional pizza-making dinner.

⸻

Day 5 – Enchanting Capri

Sail to the legendary island of Capri. Explore the Blue Grotto, shop the elegant boutiques, and enjoy a seaside swim.
As twilight falls, linger for a romantic dinner in Capri, savoring fresh seafood and island wines before returning to the mainland.

⸻

Day 6 – Positano’s Magic

Morning at leisure.
In the afternoon, head to Positano, with its pastel houses cascading into the sea. Meet a local artisan for a ceramic-painting experience, creating your own souvenir.
End the day with a candlelit dinner by the beach in Positano, framed by twinkling lights above.

⸻

Day 7 – The Heart of Amalfi

Spend the day in Amalfi town, visiting the grand cathedral and strolling the medieval alleys.
Join a lemon grove tour, learning about the region’s prized sfusato lemons. Taste limoncello and lemon-infused treats.
Conclude with a traditional Amalfitano dinner, celebrating the coast’s most iconic fruit.

⸻

Day 8 – La Dolce Far Niente

A full free day to enjoy as you wish — lounge by the beach, indulge in a spa treatment, or wander the village piazzas.

⸻

Day 9 – Ravello’s Romance

Morning free.
In the afternoon, ascend to Ravello, a jewel suspended between sea and sky. Stroll the gardens of Villa Rufolo or Villa Cimbrone, with views that inspired Wagner.
Evening: a refined dinner in Ravello, with the option of attending a private concert or aperitivo on the Terrazza dell’Infinito.

⸻

Day 10 – Wines of the Hills

A full-day immersion in the vineyards of Tramonti. Wander between vines, learn the art of local winemaking, and enjoy a rustic wine-pairing lunch in the countryside.
Return to the coast for a farewell toast under the stars.

⸻

Day 11 – Arrivederci

With hearts full and suitcases carrying memories, depart for Naples airport. Arrivederci Amalfi!
