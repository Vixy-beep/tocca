Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your Tocca Amalfi Coast experience

Tocca Amalfi Coast is a founder-hosted, small-group and bespoke travel experience on the Amalfi Coast. I personally curate every detail. Every stay, transfer, and experience is curated with care — blending comfort, culture, and exclusive access to local hosts that most travelers never meet. My goal is for you to simply arrive, and live la dolce vita.

Signature Journey – A beautifully curated, fixed-date itinerary (10–11 days) for an intimate group of 8–10 travelers. Every detail is pre-planned: premium stays, exclusive local experiences, private transportation, selected meals with wine, and your founder-host with you throughout.

Bespoke Journey – Fully customized to you. Dates, pace, accommodation, and experiences are tailored to your preferences — perfect for couples, families, solo travelers, or small groups wanting a private, made-to-order experience.

Your journey includes private ground transportation, curated local experiences (many of which are difficult to access without local connections), private or semi-private boat days, handpicked accommodations, select meals with wine, and 24/7 support from me and our trusted local partners.

Each journey has a "What's Included" section with full details.
